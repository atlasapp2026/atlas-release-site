Atlas Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Atlas is a collectible-card collection application. This policy describes the information Atlas uses when you use local collection features, optional account features, cloud protection/sync, and the graded-slab scanner.

Information Atlas processes

Account information. If you choose to create or use an Atlas account, Atlas processes your email address and authentication identifiers needed to sign you in. Passwordless email codes may also be used for authentication.

Collection and profile information. Atlas stores collection activity such as ownership, wishlist status, quantities, card variants, raw/graded holdings, grades, optional purchase details, storage notes, and collector-profile preferences. Collection data is stored locally by default. If you explicitly enable/link cloud protection, eligible collection data and sync metadata are transmitted to Atlas cloud services so the collection can be restored or synchronized.

Camera access. Atlas requests camera permission when you use the graded-slab scanner. Camera input is used to read a supported slab verification code. Atlas does not require location, contacts, payment, or advertising permissions.

Operational diagnostics. Atlas records privacy-filtered operational events such as app launches, session duration, screen categories, aggregate collection-change counts, artwork failures, and error diagnostics. The current implementation stores this operational telemetry on the device; it is not sent to a third-party analytics platform.

Cloud and service providers

Atlas uses Supabase for optional authentication, account protection, account deletion, and cloud synchronization. When those features are used, Supabase processes the account and sync data required to provide them. Atlas also connects to card-catalog and artwork providers to retrieve collectible metadata and images; those network services receive standard connection information needed to answer the request.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the collection tracker, authenticate accounts, protect and restore collections, synchronize linked collections, resolve sync conflicts, display card/catalog information, scan supported slab codes, and diagnose application reliability.

Data sharing

Atlas does not include advertising SDKs and the current source does not include a third-party analytics SDK. Data is shared with service providers only as needed to provide the requested network, authentication, cloud-sync, catalog, or artwork functionality.

Retention and deletion

Local collection and diagnostic information remains on the device until it is changed or removed by the user or the app is removed/reset. Cloud-linked data remains associated with the Atlas account as needed to provide cloud protection and synchronization. Atlas provides an account-deletion flow for deleting the cloud account and associated Atlas cloud data.

Security

Atlas uses authenticated sessions for cloud features and is designed so client builds use publishable client credentials rather than server/service-role credentials. Cloud access is subject to server-side access controls.

Children

Atlas is a general collectible-card utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through advertising or profiling features.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Atlas features or service providers change. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes.

Account deletion

A separate Atlas account-deletion page is prepared for users who need to request or complete deletion outside the app. Its public URL will be published together with this privacy policy before store submission.

Release note: This page reflects Atlas 0.9.0 build 19 source behavior validated on September 27, 2026.
